## Removed repetition

The old Start, 11:11 and continuation essays become one short introduction. Interview reflections and research/horizon subpages are consolidated by topic, not concatenated. Aura and network subpages become focused sections. The homepage no longer repeats the contact story, wider archive, research introduction and closing invitation.

Related-card blocks and the large footer invitation are removed everywhere. Documents are linked in context rather than appended as repeated source lists. No original source file or repository snapshot was deleted.

The 12 transcript chapters now share one searchable reader with chapter anchors and original video timestamps. The 24 project wrappers were redundant descriptions of directory records; their original GitHub, public website and archive links are now directly in the directory. Documents retain their previews and downloads on optional reader pages.
