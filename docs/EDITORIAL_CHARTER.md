# Editorial charter

## Reading structure, 15 September 2026

Every piece of information must earn its place. Give each subject one canonical section. Do not repeat the contact story, mythology, city ambition, research urgency or welcome across successive pages. Use an inline link when the reader needs a related source, not another page restating the same information.

Keep the main journey to the eight currently selected stops. Add a page only for a genuinely distinct purpose requested by Luke; there is no word quota to fill. Documents, transcript chapters and repository records are optional references, not compulsory steps. Do not restore the Follow another thread cards, repeated source-footnote lists or footer invitation.

Current editorial copy and route consolidations live in `src/data/journey.mjs`. The shorter structure supersedes the original requirement to traverse every archive page with Next. Preserve the direct personal voice below while cutting repetition.

Luke's direction on 11 September 2026 supersedes the earlier planning language.

The site is a boldly optimistic, joyful and humble invitation to explore Luke's work and synchronicities. Joyful Responsible Abundance is the guiding expression. Write in Luke's first-person voice for his ideas, wishes and questions. Preserve his ambition without diminishing it, conferring authority, speaking for others or turning possibilities into promises.
