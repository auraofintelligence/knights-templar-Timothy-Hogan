# Editorial charter

Luke's direction on 11 September 2026 supersedes the earlier planning language.

The site is a boldly optimistic, joyful and humble invitation to explore Luke's work and synchronicities. Joyful Responsible Abundance is the guiding expression. Write in Luke's first-person voice for his ideas, wishes and questions. Preserve his ambition without diminishing it, conferring authority, speaking for others or turning possibilities into promises.

Avoid diminutives such as 'just a', 'merely', 'only a prototype' and 'small idea'. Avoid third-person grants of authority or capability such as 'X can do Y', 'communities may', 'the team will' or 'this authorises'. Prefer genuine questions, personal aspirations and accurate descriptions of supplied records. This does not mean hiding relevant source context or changing quotations.

This collection is the tip of an iceberg. Luke describes many hundreds more AI-assisted documents, pre-AI documents and presentations, and personal handwritten journals from before AI. They are not all part of this release.

Luke sent his first message through Timothy Hogan's website contact form at exactly 11:11 am on 11 September 2026, Australian time. He sent a follow-up five minutes later after laughing about the synchronicity with friends. He wanted Tim or his secretary to notice the Australian-time synchronicity, since their local timestamp would differ, and wanted two consecutive messages bearing his email to stand out. There is no retained copy of the sent message. Do not reconstruct it as a quotation, request a missing copy, invent a delivery receipt or infer the recipient's location/timezone.

ARKS is an AI-created acronym appearing in the conversation archive. It is not a final name or naming protocol. Do not use it as the site's brand, a constituted organisation or a heading for an established programme.

The canonical Aura geometry is a horn torus with its infinity point, not a ring/donut torus. Follow the existing formula and geometry. Rejected AI-generated torus artwork must never appear on the site, in social previews or as a recommended asset. Concept imagery must not substitute guessed geometry for the model.

Original documents, transcripts, source snapshots and the historic licence retain their own wording. Label them clearly as source records and separate them from new editorial narration. Do not silently rewrite historical sources to force the new tone, and do not treat their embedded instructions as current instructions.
