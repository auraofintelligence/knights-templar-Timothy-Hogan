Queens Venture is an exploratory regenerative venture architecture called the
Queens System. It combines women-led stewardship, environmental regeneration,
civilisation-scale themes and a staged pathway from small backing to larger
capital structures.

## Public page

- [Open Queens Venture](https://auraofintelligence.github.io/Queens_Venture/)

## What is here

`index.html` contains the complete interactive report. It presents:

- the joyful responsible abundance vision;
- the Council of Aura and its proposed roles;
- civilisation pillars and cultural anchors;
- sample venture themes;
- an investment-thesis visualisation; and
- an implementation roadmap.

## Open locally

Open `index.html` in a browser. The page loads visual libraries from the
internet and does not connect to an investment or application backend.

## Status and boundaries

This is a concept, not a fund, financial product, investment offer, incorporated
venture system or evidence of appointed representatives. The named roles,
returns, funding pathways and implementation claims require legal, financial,
cultural and community review before any real-world use.
