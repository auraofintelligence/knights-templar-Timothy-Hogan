Eight useful moves

A production path with open side doors.

Each move creates a small Markdown record that can travel between people and future agents without publishing private material.

1

Frame the film

Open Film Profile. Record the promise, audience, makers, represented people, gaps and public/private posture.

2

Choose one live question

Use Research Angle for one act or claim. A narrow question is easier to source and film.

3

Leave a source trail

Use Source Trail to record what a source supports, what it does not and where it points next.

4

Invite voices with care

Use Character / Subject and Interview Questions to consider agency, context and who is missing.

5

Make three proof scenes

Try the Gumpi gateway, the sand-to-infrastructure tree and the generation city under its hardest threat load. Together they can test the whole build sequence before a long shoot.

6

Analyse what the scene proves

Use Scene Analysis to separate evidence, implication, emotion, film language and uncertainty.

7

Shape the 90 minutes

Use Run Sheet to test act order, breathing space, scale jumps and the return to a human choice.

8

Watch, listen, revise

Use Viewing Notes and Event Notes with a small, appropriate audience before wider release. Record disagreements and useful absences.

A first focused sprint

Prove the build sequence, not a smaller mission.

Three connected scenes can show why the first tunnel matters, where its material goes and how the same direction reaches a civilisation able to face the whole threat field.

Proof scene A

The gateway problem.

Film ferry arrivals, parking, buses, freight, pedestrians, road pressure and wildlife crossings. Draw the possible park-and-ride loop over what people already experience.

Proof scene B

The tunnel makes supply.

Follow one tested sand stream towards a block, glass sample, thermal store, reef form or erosion-control use. Show the conditions and unresolved leftovers too.

Proof scene C

The worldship test.

Move through joyful underground life, then load the system with nuclear, asteroid, geomagnetic and unknown scenarios. Keep evidence, model, disputed theory and story labels visible.

What one scene package can contain

Small records keep the big film understandable.

A scene folder can stay useful to a director, researcher, editor, participant or future agent without carrying the whole production in one place.

Story card

Act, approximate time, question, emotional turn, possible opening and exit image.

Source card

Claim, source, date, place, evidence label, support, limits and next check.

People card

Role, agency, invitation status, consent boundary, context, access and follow-up.

Production card

Location, sound, light, gear, archive, simulation, safety, permissions and backup idea.
