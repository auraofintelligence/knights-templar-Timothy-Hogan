# Project instructions

Use Australian English and plain community-facing language.

This repo is a public, multi-page planning site for a possible 90-minute documentary. It is a treatment and source map, not a finished film, emergency guide, development proposal or claim of authority.

## Tone

- Keep the voice invitational, curious and grounded.
- Avoid authoritarian, command-like or sweeping wording.
- Offer pathways people can enter, pause, change or decline.
- Gamify preparation, learning and contribution; do not gamify suffering, trauma or disaster.
- Keep the human and ecological subject visible when the story moves toward large systems or deep time.

## Structure

- Keep the public site multi-page.
- Preserve back-to-top and previous/next navigation.
- Keep the 90-minute total explicit when act times change.
- Keep the repo field map curated. Do not turn it into a dump of the whole repo garden.

## Evidence and boundaries

- Treat the Strange But True Field Library as relationship evidence, not current proof.
- Return consequential claims to suitable primary, scientific, cultural, government, organisational or first-hand sources.
- Keep evidence labels visible: Established, Source-backed local, Modelled, Disputed, Speculative, Story and Needs checking.
- Keep proposals, simulations, mythology and science fiction distinct from established or source-backed claims.
- Do not present the site as official Quandamooka, QYAC, emergency-service, government, scientific or partner material.
- Cultural material, people, private information, protected locations and permissions stay within their proper approval boundaries.

## Public files

- `index.html` - public doorway
- `runtime.html` - seven-act treatment
- `field-map.html` and `assets/js/field-map.js` - curated repo map
- `playbook.html` - optional game language
- `production.html` - documentary-builder pathway
- `boundaries.html` - public, cultural, evidence, privacy and safety posture
- `docs/` - readable Markdown planning records

Run JavaScript syntax checks, internal-link checks, tone-word checks and `git diff --check` before publishing. Recheck GitHub Pages after its build completes.
