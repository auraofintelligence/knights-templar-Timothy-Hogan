Safety culture is part of the build.

The workshop layer should be welcoming and serious: induction, dust control, tool checks, child/youth safeguards, privacy and respectful permission.

  • Do not improvise with hazardous materials.
  • Do not turn learning into pressure.
  • Do not skip a boring safety note because the story is exciting.
