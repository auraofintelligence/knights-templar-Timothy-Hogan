A future lived, not merely predicted

2045 is a world where love, care, work, learning and responsibility move through wider circles. Family is larger than a two-person pressure vessel. Intelligence develops in relationship. Technology strengthens human agency. Planetary repair becomes daily life.

This site moves between intimate mornings and civilisation-scale systems, while keeping original vision, active public work and future proposals clearly distinct.

Family is infrastructure.

Expecting one person to be lover, co-parent, provider, confidant, domestic partner and lifelong match has become a narrow doorway into family life. Group marriage opens more possible shapes.

The proposal is unapologetically in favour of self-sovereign adults forming larger committed family constellations. Each constellation defines its own love, parenting, homes, money, privacy and responsibilities.

A long-running plan tested through worldbuilding

Luke Nathan Hayes has developed his future plan since 2010. In 2022, he adapted part of that existing vision for the Future of Life Institute Worldbuilding Contest. It did not make the finalists, but it was a fascinating experiment. The original workbook and two day-in-the-life stories now act as historical source material, not proof that every projected event occurred.
