The register / everything, spelled out

Everything I build, on one page.

Sixteen projects, laid out plainly: where each one lives, how real it is today, whether it travels, and whether it's a sure thing or still an idea I'm working on. This is the simple version everything else on this site is drawn from, and I update it as things move.

How I sort things

Four simple questions.

Every project on this page answers the same four questions. I'm still working out if these are the right questions; more on that on the workbench page.

01

Where

Local, Australia, our region, or the whole world: where each project naturally belongs, even when it also travels further than that.

02

How real is it

Proven (it's working right now and you can see it for yourself), active (it's running and still growing), or an idea (I haven't built it yet, on purpose; not built doesn't mean forgotten, it means I'm waiting for the right time or the right hands to help).

03

Does it travel

Stays put (only works where it is, like the market stall) or travels (like music and writing, which go wherever you take them). This matters more the further from home a project sits.

04

Gold or purple

Gold and solid means it's proven, real, something you can check for yourself. Purple and dashed means it's still my own idea, open to being argued with, changed, or knocked back altogether.

What's not on this table, and why

The workshop goes deeper than this page.

Behind these sixteen sit around 110 more projects: experiments, sketches, half-written songs, ideas still in a drawer. This table carries the ones ready to stand in front of strangers; the rest come off the shelf in later passes. And there are a few pieces of my public work I'm still deciding whether to show at this particular door: that call lives on the workbench, where I keep everything I haven't decided yet.

Built in the open: gold is what's real today, purple is what's still becoming. Back something here and something real comes back.
