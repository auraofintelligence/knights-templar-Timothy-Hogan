The person under the portfolio

Hands first, then systems, always songs.

Amity Point has held my family for generations. The rest of me was built the long way round.

What my hands know

I learned brakes and steering in my dad's workshop, set up stages at Glastonbury, delivered meals-on-wheels, and won my school's orienteering title by cutting my own path. These days I build calm software. The through-line hasn't changed: practical help, no fuss.

Four fronts, one person

Strange but True at the stall. Luke Catalyst in the systems world. Aura of Intelligence holding the long arc. i C. infinity for everything that needs to be sung.

Still becoming
