How the money moves

Fast lane or fee-light lane: your call.

Card or wallet checkout is quickest. PayID, bank transfer and cash dodge the processor fees, but I check and fulfil those by hand most days: delivery waits until the money lands, and I'd rather tell you that than pretend. Crypto rails are on the bench for later; never send funds to an address you haven't verified with me directly.

Money, tax and fee specifics get checked against current Australian rules before anything is charged; none of this is financial advice. The working terms and rails live at my stall, Strange but True.
