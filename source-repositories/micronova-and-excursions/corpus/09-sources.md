## A note on reading the frontier's citations

The books cite real papers with real DOIs, and a reader can check them. Some support what they are said to support. Some are accurate about a finding and then carry more interpretive weight than the paper claims. Some are correct in the citation and wrong in the summary.

Checking them one at a time is slow and it is the only way to know which is which. That work is not done here, and doing it would be the most valuable next contribution this repo could hold.
