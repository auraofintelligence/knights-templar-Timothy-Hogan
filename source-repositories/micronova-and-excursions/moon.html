The magnetotail test, and the tilt.

The monthly passage through the magnetic tail is an earthquake question, and it is argued as one.

The Moon crosses the Earth's magnetic tail once a month, geomagnetic activity has been measured running a few per cent low before full moon and a few per cent high after, and tidal stress has a published relationship with earthquakes. The discriminator falls out of that: gravity predicts a symmetric month, the magnetotail predicts a lopsided one, and a flat residual kills it. Earthquake prediction →
