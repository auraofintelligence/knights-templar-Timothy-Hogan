Evidence language

Five labels used across the site.

“Official” does not automatically mean independent. A manufacturer’s product page is strong evidence for what it claims to sell, but performance and openness claims still need testing or licence inspection.

Established evidence

Publicly supported

Supported within the stated scope by a primary authority, standard, law, official record or repeatable technical evidence. This is not a claim of permanent or universal proof.

Vendor claim

Self-described

Useful for product features and published specifications; not treated as an independent comparison.

Working experiment

Live route

Available components and tools invite exploration now. Start here, elsewhere or in several places at once.

Research

Testable and unresolved

Models, prototypes and partner programmes turn open questions into fresh evidence.

Exploration frontier

Open question

Maths, simulation, architecture and experiments begin now. Unresolved science, capital, access and law remain part of the terrain around physical deployment.

Correction welcome

Versioned evidence

Better primary sources, measurements and independent critiques reshape every living claim.

Plain-English bridge

An open glossary.

These working explanations open the technical language; the linked sources lead deeper.

Arm

An instruction-set and processor-design family widely used in phones, embedded systems and efficient servers. Arm licenses designs; it does not manufacture the finished chip.

FPGA

Reconfigurable logic: a chip whose hardware arrangement changes after manufacture. It tests ideas, evolves indefinitely or remains the chosen deployed architecture in its own right.

ASIC

An application-specific integrated circuit: custom silicon built for a defined job, rather than a general-purpose processor.

SoC

A system-on-chip: CPU, memory interfaces, accelerators, security and other functions combined in one package or die.

Tape-out

The point where one chosen version of a chip layout is released for mask preparation and fabrication. Errors after this handoff make a respin expensive.

PDK + EDA

A process design kit contains foundry rules and models. Electronic design automation tools turn and check a design against those rules.

DTN

Delay-tolerant networking stores data until a link becomes available. This is essential when connections are intermittent or separated by space-time delay.

CDU

A coolant distribution unit moves and controls liquid between hot computing equipment and the system that rejects or reuses the heat.

Web3 Sensorium

Here: a federated evidence network using portable identity, signed provenance and shared rules. It does not mean placing raw sensor streams or private data on a public blockchain.
