Overview/Governance compact

A living compact for community evolution

Life, people, Country and the commons shape the purpose.

Open systems for open minds pair public code with agency, responsibility, repair, exit and institutions shaped to keep the commons in common hands.

Purpose lock

Increase human and ecological agency without concentrating invisible power.

Promises that keep evolving

  • Aura serves people, Country and living systems before financial extraction.
  • Important decisions remain legible, contestable and human-accountable.
  • People export their data, leave the system and keep essential functions.
  • Open interfaces make substitution, repair and exit more feasible, while patents, certification, hosting and supply-chain dependencies remain visible.
  • Speculation remains welcome when it is labelled and testable.
  • Revenue sustains the work; scientific truth, personal data and community consent stay outside purchase.

Rights of a participant

Every person keeps a door out.

Consent evolves with context. It stays specific across personal reflection, shared research, community data and public outputs. Withdrawal shapes future collection and sharing. Records already lawfully published or distributed follow their applicable retention and distribution context.

Know

See what is sensed, inferred, stored, shared and used to make a recommendation.

Choose

Accept, reject or defer suggestions without losing unrelated essential functions.

Correct

Challenge an inference, add context and preserve disagreement beside the model.

Leave

Export data in usable formats, revoke future sharing and use an interoperable alternative.

Fractal governance

Local agency inside a global purpose.

The same values repeat at different scales, while agency remains grounded in people and place. GAJRA Earth is the global association and purpose layer; Ready S.E.T. is the smaller, place-based co-operative fractal. Each layer publishes its chosen scope, shared interfaces and exit paths.

Person

Self + household

Own consent, private data, personal devices and the right to ignore a recommendation.

Place

Ready S.E.T. Co-operative

The proposed Sustainable Employment and Training fractal also reads as “ready, set, go”. It turns local learning, maintenance and services into member-governed work. It is not yet a registered co-operative.

Planet

GAJRA Earth

The proposed Global Association for Joyful Responsible Abundance on Earth holds shared purpose, open federation rules and planetary coordination. It is not yet a registered global association.

Authority

Country + public institutions

Traditional Owner governance, regulators, emergency agencies and other lawful authorities retain the decisions that belong to them.

Freedom + consequence

Self-sovereign exploration. Shared responsibility at shared interfaces.

Self-sovereign people simulate, fork, model, fabricate and choose their own direction. Responsibility joins the path when work materially affects other people, shared infrastructure, Country, the environment or public systems.

Self-sovereign

Explore, fork and build

Think, simulate, write code, rewire an owned FPGA, fabricate silicon and run voluntary private experiments. Fork the map whenever another direction calls.

Voluntary

Choose collaborators and critics

People decide whose knowledge, testing and challenge will sharpen their work. Expertise informs choice; it does not own curiosity.

Shared consequence

Take responsibility for effects on others

When field sensing, electrical systems, food processes, machinery or public claims affect other people, builders obtain the relevant consent and bring appropriate competence and risk controls.

Country

Traditional Owner authority

Traditional Owners hold decisions over their cultural knowledge and access to Country. Physical work follows those relationships.

Personal

Individual consent

Each person chooses the path of their private, health and biological data. Boards, DAOs, experts and agents hold no substitute for that choice.

Public role

Name the role being claimed

Operational warnings, medical claims, spectrum, launch and public construction carry defined legal roles. Personal experiments remain free; public services name their operator, evidence and accountability.

Funding relationships

Money funds work. Truth stays unpriced.

  • Publish material funders and conflicts.
  • Separate scientific review from sponsorship decisions.
  • Personal, health, cultural and biological data remains outside sale.
  • Core household and safety functions stay free from subscription lock-in.
  • Budget for security updates, repair, end-of-life and data exit.
  • Return a defined share of surplus to the commons and participating communities.

Institution still to design

The legal form is an open question.

Possible structures span a co-operative, incorporated not-for-profit, charitable research body, public-benefit company, stewardship trust or a carefully separated combination. Each carries different fundraising, liability, tax, member-control and asset-lock implications.

A DAO token offers one coordination tool. Law, fiduciary duty, Country authority, practical competence and meaningful participation remain part of the wider institutional design.

Material funding and sensitive data bring Australian legal, tax, privacy and governance expertise into the design. Publish the constitution that grows from that work.

Living anti-capture questions

Keep asking as the commons evolves.

The answers reveal how power moves, even while the code remains on GitHub.

Do participants keep essential functions while withholding unrelated data?
Data collection stays proportional to the function. Optional research sharing remains separate and reversible.
Does an independent team reproduce the key result?
Publish the data, code, configuration, uncertainties and negative tests within the project’s legal, ethical and cultural relationships.
Does a community or Traditional Owner body hold a real stop mechanism?
A genuine stop mechanism begins with the relationship; consultation without it becomes theatre.
Does the system keep working when one company, cloud or founder disappears?
Portable formats, open interfaces, local operation, documented recovery and multiple maintainers support continuity.
Does funding reshape the question while results stay independent?
Funders commission transparent questions. Methods, findings and dissent remain protected from commercial veto.
