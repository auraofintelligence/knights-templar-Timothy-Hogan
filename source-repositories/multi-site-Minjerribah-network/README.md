## Purpose

This is a public workbench for 7 Mile and other Minjerribah site and service ideas. It brings together the information supplied so far, open questions, related public projects and source trails, including a site-neutral Sand and Screen proposal.

The subjects include living, cultural and research residencies, short stays, recovery, safety, housing, aged care, longevity, rejuvenation research, technology, making, repair, training and community wealth.

The website is not an organisation or decision-making body. Participation, permissions, site access and cultural decisions sit with the relevant people.

## 7 Mile working reference

The current site description is approximately **15 acres**.

The supplied Google Maps trace follows the existing firebreak and records:

- **54,281.67 square metres**
- **5.43 hectares**
- **13.4 acres**
- **1.02 kilometres** around the measured path

The trace is not a surveyed property boundary. The wider claimed area is larger.

The 7 Mile capacity tiles use a Gemini Flash land-use guestimate. It treats the full working map area as usable for one arithmetic exercise. Its footprints, bay counts and allocations are illustrative rather than surveyed or design-confirmed figures.

The current arithmetic guestimate explores:

- 70 container-based buildings with shared kitchens, cold storage, amenities, decks and shaded aprons
- about 65 flexible places for vans, swags, tents, caravans, rigs, buses and house trucks
- sandy access tracks and vehicle loops
- ceremony and cultural grounds, food forest, gardens, nursery, commons, water harvesting and a Vertical Tower Farm and Seedling Hub

This is a discussion exercise, not a final layout or commitment.

## Funding and organisational pathways

- **Country, family and cultural decisions:** Site invitation, culture, ceremony, restricted knowledge and cultural decisions sit with the relevant people. Country is not a company shareholding or charity asset.
- **7 Mile commercial proposal:** The current 41 per cent, 39 per cent and 20 per cent working split concerns suitable commercial activity at 7 Mile. It does not automatically extend to another site.
- **Purpose-specific not-for-profit bodies:** Different charitable purposes and services may require different organisations, boards, skills and reporting.
- **Ready S.E.T. Co-op:** Ready Sustainable Employment and Training Cooperative remains hypothetical. Its possible field includes employment, training, members, shared equipment, transport and administration between willing participants.
- **Strange But True:** Strange But True: Tech, art and ideas that'll help is the existing sole-trader business supporting research, technology, websites, planning and communication.
- **C-Hour and community wealth:** These remain experimental pathways for recognising voluntary contribution, sharing assets and retaining value locally.

## Source handling

The public source page distinguishes supplied maps and local descriptions from accessible public project pages. The source document library makes the supplied submissions, planning drafts, research conversations and handover material available in their original file formats. Publication does not convert a proposal into an agreement, a planning idea into a decision or an old research lead into a verified current fact.

## Design and accessibility

- Atkinson Hyperlegible body text and Nunito Sans headings match the related public sites.
- All regular text is at least one rem, with a large base size for older readers.
- Every public page has a distinct labelled GenAI hero until suitable local photographs are available.
- The solid menu lists the 14 main destinations, with Sand and Screen directly after 7 Mile. The source document library is linked from the Source Trail and full site map.
- Glass and metallic surfaces, gentle hero light, small parallax, magnetic buttons and one-time reveals provide a shared visual language.
- Reduced-motion settings remove non-essential animation.
