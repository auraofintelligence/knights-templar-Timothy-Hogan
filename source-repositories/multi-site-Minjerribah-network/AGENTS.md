# Local working instructions

## Purpose

This repository presents a public working proposal for distinct community opportunities on Minjerribah. Each place has its own purpose, people, authority and participation decision. Selected capabilities may be shared only where the relevant people choose that relationship.

## Voice

- Use Australian English and plain language.
- Always write Ready S.E.T. Co-op, never Ready S.E.T. without Co-op.
- Always use the Noonuccal spelling.
- Do not name private individuals.
- Use neutral, invitational language without pretending proposals are approved.
- Avoid the word "can" where it sounds like permission or authority being granted.
- Do not rank 7 Mile above other sites or describe it as the lead, heart, centre or owner of a network.
- Keep 7 Mile first in the main navigation because it is the immediate planning conversation, not because it governs another place.
- Keep known facts, working proposals and future possibilities visibly separate.
- Do not claim to speak for families, cultural authorities, organisations, service providers, government or landholders.
- Public copy describes the opportunity itself, not why a page, card or layout was created.
- Do not repeat the same authority or participation caveat in every section. Keep the shortest useful safeguard where it changes how a specific proposal should be understood.
- Every paragraph must add a fact, distinction, open question or useful route. Remove filler and repeated summaries.

## Boundaries

- Country does not become an asset of a company, charity or cooperative.
- Operating on a site does not transfer ownership of Country or cultural authority.
- Ready S.E.T. Co-op is proposed and is not currently registered.
- Strange But True is the existing sole-trader seed business.
- The proposed 41/39/20 Indigenous-majority company is initially and predominantly a 7 Mile vehicle. It does not automatically extend to other sites.
- The 13.4 acre figure is a working map measurement inside the 7 Mile firebreak, not a surveyed boundary.
- The 7 Mile full-site land budget is a Gemini Flash guestimate for arithmetic discussion only. Do not present its footprints, bay counts or allocations as hard numbers, surveyed findings or a decided layout.
- Sand and Screen remains site neutral. The newer former sand-loading area and 10 to 12 Ballow Road are separate location leads, and neither is presented as selected.
- 9 Ballow Road is a separate Ready S.E.T. Co-op public-doorway proposal, not a Sand and Screen site.
- 82 Claytons Road is a separate future possibility awaiting more information.
- Do not imply clinical treatment, property access, planning approval, funding readiness or organisational participation.

## Visual system

- Distinct from the Cultural Intelligence Node while clearly related through cross-links.
- Deep paper-bark charcoal, warm sand, lagoon blue, sunrise coral and leaf green.
- Canvas and CSS may carry abstract network visuals. They are not maps or surveyed site plans.
- Meaningful content remains visible without JavaScript.
- Motion respects reduced-motion settings.

## Navigation and checks

- The solid all-pages menu links to all 14 main destinations, with Home first, then 7 Mile, then Sand and Screen, followed by every detail and context page.
- Each major theme has a substantial page of its own.
- Every public page appears in the human-readable site map.
- Every public page has previous and next navigation and a return-to-top control.
- External links open in a new tab with secure relationship attributes.
- Run `npm run check` before publishing.
