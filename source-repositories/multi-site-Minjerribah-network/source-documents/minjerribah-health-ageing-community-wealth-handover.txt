# Neutral handover context: Minjerribah health, ageing, rejuvenation, community wealth and 82 Claytons Road

This material describes a **concurrent possibility space**, not a settled masterplan. Several sites, organisations, technologies, financing structures and research pathways are being explored at the same time. Some have existing organisations or assets behind them, some have expressions of interest or conversations underway, and some remain longer-horizon possibilities. Nothing here should be read as assigning a role to another organisation, declaring consent, or assuming participation.

## The health, ageing and rejuvenation field

A distributed Minjerribah health and ageing research ecosystem is being explored alongside the other community-site projects.

Current conversations and source material touch NSI Housing, MMEIC, Yulu Burri Ba, Moopi Moopi Pa, 82 Claytons Road at Amity, future Point Lookout possibilities, Anglican Church Southern Queensland and Anglicare Southern Queensland.

The interest is in what becomes possible when different real-world settings participate concurrently in their own ways: independent housing, community health, Elders and aged care, cultural and community settings, specialist research infrastructure, private longevity services, home-based digital systems and larger aged-care networks.

Any involvement by an organisation would arise through that organisation's own decisions, agreements, clinical responsibilities, cultural authority, property arrangements and data permissions.

The technical research field includes healthy ageing, dementia, cognition, reablement, mobility, sleep, nutrition, social connection, carer wellbeing, environmental conditions, rehabilitation, biological-age measurement, longitudinal digital twins and emerging rejuvenation technologies.

The existing Aura material already contains three useful technical domains:

**Aura Clinical** concerns the body/mind digital twin, longitudinal physiological, cognitive, behavioural and narrative information, and clinical or research applications.

**Aura Place** concerns the person's physical environment, including the home or care setting, sensors, environmental information, routines, safety, autonomy, nutrition and ageing in place.

**Aura Access** concerns shared access to more specialised health and longevity equipment rather than requiring every participant or household to own the capital equipment. 

The broader Aura Geode work adds multimodal sensing, wearables, environmental data, personal data vaults, federated learning, longitudinal protocol development and a 60-session/120-hour research architecture. 

The intended research architecture is not fixed around one present-day modality. HBOT, thermal interventions, photobiomodulation, ultrasound, neuromodulation, diagnostics, metabolic interventions, regenerative medicine, biomaterials and future rejuvenation technologies all sit within the wider experimental field where suitable research, engineering, ethics and regulatory pathways exist.

The clinical-development material already contemplates progression from feasibility and usability work through single-site pilots and then multi-site studies where appropriate. 

## 82 Claytons Road, Amity

82 Claytons Road is a distinct project within that wider field.

The historical property research records:

**Address:** 82 Claytons Road, Amity QLD 4183
**Lot:** 23SL3665
**Area:** 13,689 m² / 1.3689 ha
**Land status:** Current
**Recorded planning designation:** CF, Community Facilities, Precinct CF5
**Registered owner identified through the paid title search:** The Corporation of the Synod of the Diocese of Brisbane.  

The working site history describes an older church being moved to the property after erosion affected its former location, later suffering termite damage and eventually being removed. That history should not be converted into an assumption that the present site itself is actively eroding or uniquely termite-prone.

Older AI-generated documents also made strong claims about overlays, development difficulty, low value and "problem asset" status. Those are not adopted as conclusions here. Present planning controls, overlay extents, physical conditions and development pathways require current verification against official sources.

The current conceptual use of 82 Claytons is much broader than the old "Aura clinic" framing. It is being explored as a **longevity, wellbeing, research, learning, art/science and technology site**, potentially including Aura of Intelligence, digital twins, space-weather simulation, public learning, film, outdoor cinema, practitioner training, specialist equipment and cooperative health research.

The site also has a potential institutional role because the title holder and Anglicare sit within a much larger Anglican social-service ecosystem. Earlier research specifically explored connecting Aura dementia and wellbeing work into Anglicare networks. 

No Anglican support, site access, property transfer, planning approval or Anglicare participation is assumed.

## Property transfer and community wealth

One active possibility explored in the source material is **donation of 82 Claytons Road into an island-serving asset structure**, rather than treating ordinary commercial purchase as the only pathway.

Earlier work explicitly contemplated donation of the property into a Straddie Sovereign Wealth Fund structure. 

A separate historical point appearing in the source conversations is that the Diocese may itself have received the property without purchasing it. That point should remain **a historical claim requiring documentary verification** before appearing as a public factual statement or negotiating assertion.

The strategic interest in donation is wider than simply obtaining land. It opens a question about whether an underused institutional asset could become part of a longer-lived island community-wealth base while the Diocese remains involved through service, research, care, finance or other mutually agreed relationships.

The **Straddie Sovereign Wealth Fund / Civilisation of Sand** research explores a broader model in which locally generated value from energy, infrastructure, materials, ecological work and enterprises is retained and compounded for long-term community benefit rather than continually leaving the island. 

The **Moreton Bay Community Wealth and Mutuals** work adds another branch of that thinking: mutuals, cooperatives, shared assets and community wealth do not have to be one entity or one financial instrument. They form a family of possible ownership and stewardship structures.

The health-tech work contributes a similar idea at smaller scale: expensive health infrastructure may be collectively financed and shared through cooperative structures rather than being available only through individual ownership or conventional private clinics. 

C-Hour and the Braided Economy sit alongside ordinary money rather than replacing it. Recent source material describes a three-account logic: money, voluntary contribution and public value remain distinguishable while informing each other. 

## Concurrent sites and organisations

The wider health and ageing work presently includes exploratory intersections with:

**NSI Housing**, around housing, ageing in place, home adaptation, independent living and digital-twin-of-place possibilities.

**Yulu Burri Ba**, around possible intersections with primary health, community health, Indigenous health, prevention, ageing and dementia.

**Moopi Moopi Pa**, around possible aged-care, Elders, quality-of-life, cognition, mobility, social connection and reablement research. The Ready S.E.T. concept material already identifies Moopi Moopi Pa as a possible aged-care and Elders connection while explicitly preserving separate leadership and responsibilities. 

**MMEIC**, around possible cultural, community, education, Elders and intergenerational dimensions.

**82 Claytons Road**, around specialist research, longevity, learning, advanced wellbeing, technology and Anglican/Anglicare relationships.

**A future Point Lookout site**, around advanced ageing, premium longevity, rejuvenation, specialist practitioners, higher-comfort residential or retreat-style services and private-pay demand. This remains a future possibility rather than an identified committed property.

**Anglicare Southern Queensland**, around possible research translation, aged care, home care, dementia, workforce development and eventual wider deployment of useful systems beyond Minjerribah.

The existence of these possibilities should not be turned into statements that any organisation has accepted a role.

## State-level pathway

A separate conversation introduced Queensland's broadened **State strategic project** mechanism as a possible higher-level pathway for integrated projects crossing ordinary departmental boundaries.

Its relevance is not that 82 Claytons requires State strategic project status. The relevance is that a future Minjerribah system could potentially present health, housing, transport, digital twins, advanced materials, energy, environmental works, community wealth, research and infrastructure as interdependent work packages rather than unrelated unusual projects.

That remains an additional pathway in the possibility space.
