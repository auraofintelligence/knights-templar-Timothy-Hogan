The 72-hour launch sequence

Spark → recheck → choose → go.

  1. Capture the spark. Invitation, person, event, instinct or unexpected opening.
  2. Shortlist routes. Entry, activity permission, host action, lead time and fallback.
  3. Recheck official claims. Refresh the time-sensitive claim IDs instead of trusting an old whole-country review.
  4. Confirm the host. Duties, payment, venue, paperwork and what changes if the plan grows.
  5. Keep one way out. Reversible bookings, onward travel, backup communications and a realistic alternative.
  6. Choose. The atlas proposes; the traveller decides and commits.
