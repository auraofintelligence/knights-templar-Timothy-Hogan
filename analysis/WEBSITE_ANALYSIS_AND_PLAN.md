# Timothy Hogan, Aura and the work ahead

## Website analysis and proposed build plan

Prepared for Luke Nathan Hayes on 11 September 2026. Status: for review before building.

This is a plan for the place Timothy Hogan and his team can visit if they respond to Luke. It is not a new outreach message or campaign. The contact has already happened, according to Luke's account. The website should introduce the person behind that contact, explain the connection to his existing work, and make the supporting material easy to explore.

Repository: [knights-templar-Timothy-Hogan](https://github.com/auraofintelligence/knights-templar-Timothy-Hogan).

Planned website address: [auraofintelligence.github.io/knights-templar-Timothy-Hogan/](https://auraofintelligence.github.io/knights-templar-Timothy-Hogan/). This address is a deployment target, not a claim that the site is live. The remote repository was checked during this review: public, empty, no About description or homepage URL. Nothing has been committed, pushed or published by this planning work.

## 1. The recommendation

Build a cinematic, source-connected, multi-page introduction and research archive. Its central story is the meeting of an established body of work with a new conversation, not the creation of an ecosystem around a podcast.

The homepage needs to answer five questions quickly:

1. Who is Luke?
2. Why did this interview matter to him?
3. What did he contact Tim about?
4. What relevant work can Tim actually inspect?
5. Where could a conversation go from here?

My recommended working title is **Timothy Hogan, Aura and the work ahead**. The shorter navigation identity can be **Aura: The meeting point**. Neither should suggest that Luke operates Tim's organisation or represents the Knights Templar. The repository name can remain exactly as supplied.

The public voice should be Luke's first-person voice when explaining his intentions, with clear attribution when discussing the interview and third-person descriptions for source records. Warm, intellectually adventurous and specific. No manufactured institutional voice, no generic fundraising pitch and no implication that a reply, invitation or access agreement already exists.

The design ambition is an exhibition-quality experience with the depth of a research library. Spacious entrances, purposeful motion, original slides, atmospheric raster imagery, readable essays and unusually good connections between evidence and ideas. Awards cannot be promised; this is the quality bar we can design and test against.

### 3.3 The original Aura work is more specific than a general spiritual theme

D01 joins self-reflection to social participation and a proposed personal operating system. D02 then supplies a concrete visual vocabulary: a 12 by 24 structure, seven coloured shells, inside/outside distinctions, mobile screens and links between personal records and wider environments.

The current W01 README states the address calculation explicitly: 12 x 24 x 7 x 2 = **4,032 face addresses**. The supplied evolution infographic says 4,002. Preserve that image as received, but correct the website's explanation and attach a short source note to the image record. Do not reproduce the typo in an interactive model.

The stable geometry is an address structure. Learned associations and relationships are a different layer. This is a useful technical explanation in its own right; it does not require claiming that a geometric diagram proves consciousness, ancient technology or a physical field.

The 2025 material adds an important correction in Luke's own words: the twin relationship is not automatically a master-tool arrangement, and tools should remain removable like clothing. That belongs near the centre of the Aura explanation. Some generated responses in the same document prematurely call the capsule certified or the outcomes established. The website should describe the design and proposed research honestly, without adopting that certainty.

### 3.5 The 2026 civic documents give the network practical structure

D05 and D06 propose a continuum from personal systems through neighbourhood and community infrastructure to national and larger computing systems. Their strongest contribution is choice, local capability and resilience, not a particular chip vendor. The train, install, experiment, document and teach pattern is especially relevant to a research network.

D07 and D08 extend the idea to cooperation between distinct people and places. The useful principle is family without assimilation: shared capability while people retain their own authority, culture and right to leave.

D12's older language describes the C-Hour in financialised terms, including monetisation and a proposed asset class. Later materials distinguish ordinary money, voluntary contribution and outcome records more clearly. These are meaningful differences. The site should show the development and use the later plain-language distinction for its present explanation, while leaving the archived originals intact. It should not turn assertions about taxation or legal status in a submission into a statement of settled law.

This is directly relevant to possible artefact work: a shared archive need not mean ownership moves to Luke, and shared analysis need not mean every record becomes public. The public landing site itself will contain the approved public collection, not a private custodial database.

### 3.6 Research, simulation and culture each have a useful role

D09 asks about a specific ambitious research target: magnitude 7+ earthquakes, an hour of warning, within 100 km. It does not supply a demonstrated prediction system. The [USGS explanation of earthquake prediction](https://www.usgs.gov/faqs/can-you-predict-earthquakes?items_per_page=6&page=0) supports keeping this distinct from established forecasting and warning capabilities. Present it as a research question with possible tests, not a public alert service.

Likewise, *What Would You Choose* explicitly sets parts of its catastrophic scenario in a game/simulation context. Future settlements, worldships and historical-catastrophe hypotheses can be explored richly without being labelled as built infrastructure or confirmed forecasts.

D11 is a proposed clinical research pathway, not trial results. It offers a staged approach to testing person-centred memory and care tools. Keep health research connected to Aura's human purpose, but do not make it the entry argument for vault attendance or imply that access to an artefact could deliver a therapy.

The music, poems and image collages are not decoration pasted onto a technical project. They show why the work matters to Luke. They belong in an accessible culture section and at carefully chosen narrative transitions. Visitors should be able to experience them without being asked to treat poetic language as a laboratory result.

### 3.7 The main editorial conclusion

The strongest position is neither 'the interview proves my architecture' nor 'my projects explain every claim in the interview'. It is: **this conversation touches questions I have worked on for years, and here is enough of that work for you to see where a useful exchange might begin.**

## 7. The visual direction

### A luminous archive, grounded in a real person

Use deep ink and obsidian, warm brass/gold, restrained aurora cyan and occasional violet, with warm ivory reading surfaces. The contrast between cinematic openings and calm source-reading spaces should be deliberate. Shine should feel like light on glass, mineral and metal, rather than glow around every control.

Use a distinctive display face for major titles and a highly readable text family for essays. Self-host licensed fonts. Keep headings plain, punctuation normal and the primary language Australian English. Avoid decorative eyebrow labels, heading rules, faux-Latin ornament and a generic medieval crest.

The overall composition should connect ancient questions, present-day tools and an Australian place-based horizon. Do not create an invented image of the actual vault or an alleged artefact and let it pass as documentation. No synthetic portrait of Tim is needed. Use Luke's supplied imagery where appropriate and caption concept images as concept images.

Check final output and browser requests, including third-party components, rather than merely scanning for `.svg` filenames.

Create raster derivatives for any selected display use. This honours the no-SVG website direction without silently deleting originals from the requested archive. ## 8. Motion and interactions that earn their place

The goal is a memorable experience, not an obstacle course.

| Feature | Behaviour | Fallback and limit |
|---|---|---|
| Hero atmosphere | Slow light/material movement or shallow parallax, with crisp static text | Still image in reduced-motion or low-power mode; no autoplay sound |
| Page transitions | Short fades and continuity between related destinations | Normal browser navigation remains functional |
| Source-linked timeline | Switch between origin, document, build and future-proposal dates | Fully readable chronological list |
| Interview reader | Search phrases; jump to chapter/time; copy a timecoded link | Complete HTML transcript and downloadable original |
| Relationship explorer | Select a concept to see a small set of labelled connections | Equivalent table/list; no 152-node hairball on first load |
| Aura matrix demonstration | Rotate or change view while the selected address remains stable | Static raster views and numerical explanation; no camera permission |
| Slide and image viewer | Filmstrip, zoom, keyboard controls, source/download links | Individual preview pages and accessible text |
| Unified search | Find pages, projects, sources and transcript passages with visible result types | Human-readable site map and library index |
| Reading tools | Contents, progress, back to top, previous/next and print styles | Native links and browser print remain usable |
| Local bookmarks | Save a small reading list on the visitor's device | Optional, clearly local, reset/export available |
| Share controls | Copy a stable page/section link; native sharing when supported | Plain URL copying, no social SDK |

Focus the connection explorer on typed relationships: predates, shares a method, supplies an example, proposes a setting, or raises a question. A line must never silently mean 'proves'.

Use short transitions, stop offscreen animation and provide a persistent motion setting. Honour operating-system reduced-motion preferences.
